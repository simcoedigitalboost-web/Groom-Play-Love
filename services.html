Our Grooming Services

Professional care tailored to your dog's needs. All services performed with patience and love.

Grooming Packages

Full Groom Package

Our signature service for dogs who deserve the complete treatment. Your pup will leave looking and feeling their absolute best.

Includes: Bath with premium shampoo and conditioner, full haircut and styling, nail trim, ear cleaning, sanitary trim, brush out, blow dry, and finishing cologne.

Wash and Fluff

Perfect for maintaining your dog's coat between full grooming appointments. A thorough clean that leaves them fresh and fluffy.

Includes: Bath with premium shampoo, conditioning treatment, thorough brush out, blow dry, ear cleaning, and finishing cologne.

Mini Groom Package

A lighter touch-up for pups who need a quick refresh. Great for dogs between full grooming appointments or those who need less intensive styling.

Includes: Bath, brush out, nail trim, ear cleaning, and tidying of face, feet, and sanitary areas.

Bath Only

Ideal for short-haired breeds who need a good clean without the full grooming treatment. Simple, effective, and affordable.

Includes: Bath with premium shampoo, towel and blow dry.

Add-On Services

Enhance any grooming package with these specialty treatments for your pup.

Teeth Brushing

Freshen breath and support dental health with a gentle teeth brushing treatment.

Medicated Shampoo and Spray Treatment

Targeted treatment for dogs with skin sensitivities, allergies, or dermatological conditions.

All Natural Flea Bath

A natural solution for flea prevention and treatment, gentle on your dog's skin and coat.

Paw Treatment

Pamper tired paws with our conditioning treatment for dry and cracked pads. Includes a bubble bath paw soak, shea butter paw rub, and smooth nails.
