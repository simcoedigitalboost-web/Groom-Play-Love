At Groom Play Love Dog Salon we aim to provide the best possible environment for your canine family member. We offer a safe, clean, calm, and comfortable environment for grooming. We diffuse pure therapeutic essential oils throughout our salon to soothe nervous dogs and remove toxins from the air.

Our Grooming Services

All services provided in our calm, kennel-free environment. No kennels, no stress—just patient, attentive care from start to finish.

Full Groom Package

The complete treatment for your pup. Bath, haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning, and finishing touches to have them looking their absolute best.

Wash and Fluff

A thorough bath and blow-dry to keep your dog clean, fresh, and fluffy between full grooming appointments.

Mini Groom Package

A lighter touch-up including bath, brush out, nail trim, and tidying up the face, feet, and sanitary areas.

Bath Only

Perfect for short-haired breeds. A quality bath and dry to keep your pup clean and smelling great.

We use 100% natural products for all dog grooming services.

A regularly groomed pet makes a happy and healthy pet. Price varies and depends on breed, size of dog and condition of coat. Please feel free to contact us for specific price information.
